The Westminster Award (WA) is an employability achievement award, designed to help students gain formal recognition for the extracurricular activities they take part in during their time as a University of Westminster student. Students are awarded the Westminster Award for demonstrating commitment to their personal and professional development.

To complete the Bronze level, students may have taken part in a range of activities, including:

  • Completing a Skills Audit, considering their strengths and weaknesses and identifying avenues for development

  • Improving their digital literacy by completing a range of online courses

  • Attending events to practice networking, communication, and time management skills

  • Exploring and committing to take part in a range of Careers and Employability activities alongside their academic studies

Skills/Knowledge

  • Skills Audit
  • Digital Literacy
  • Networking Skills
  • Communication Skills
  • Time Management
